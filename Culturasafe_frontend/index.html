Location & Privacy Policy

1. Why does CulturaSafe request location?
To pinpoint your location on the Leaflet map relative to Puducherry's attractions and calculate real-time turn-by-turn driving or walking directions.

2. Is your location shared publicly?
No. Your coordinates are never made public, broadcast to other tourists, or sold. All live tracking updates stay in your browser session.

3. Cloud Storage (Opt-In Only):
Continuous location history is recorded to the backend database only if you explicitly enable the "Save history to cloud" checkbox and are signed into your account. Cloud writes are throttled to conserve battery and network bandwidth.

4. Deletion:
You can inspect and delete all stored location coordinates at any time via the "Saved History" panel.

5. Background Tracking Limitations:
Browser-based GPS tracking operates only while the web page is active. When you close or minimize the browser, or when your mobile OS suspends the tab, GPS updates pause automatically.

6. HTTPS Requirement:
The browser Geolocation API requires a secure connection (HTTPS) in production or localhost in development.
